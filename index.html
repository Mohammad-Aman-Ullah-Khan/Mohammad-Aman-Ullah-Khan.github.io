About

I'm a Computer Science graduate from BRAC University (Dhaka, Bangladesh), currently working as a Research Assistant at the AI-Centre of Excellence in Health, a nationally funded AI-in-healthcare initiative at Manipal Academy of Higher Education, supported by India's Ministry of Education. My work sits at the intersection of NLP, medical imaging, and systems that need to work reliably outside high-resource, high-data environments.

My undergraduate thesis built a Bangla dialect-to-English speech translation system for rural telemedicine, using neural machine translation to make real-time medical communication possible where standard speech models fail. Since then, I've published on hybrid CNN/attention architectures for medical imaging, and I'm currently working on privacy-preserving federated learning for brain tumor segmentation across institutions.
