Computer Science — LLMs · NLP · Clinical AI

I work on large language models, multimodal learning, and explainable AI, applied mainly to clinical and low-resource settings — from federated brain-tumor segmentation to dialect speech translation for rural telemedicine. I'm currently a Research Assistant at the AI‑Centre of Excellence in Health, a nationally funded AI-in-healthcare initiative at Manipal Academy of Higher Education, and I'm looking for a PhD group where I can push further on privacy-preserving and multilingual AI.

What I work on

  • Federated and privacy-preserving learning for multi-institution medical imaging
  • Explainable AI for diagnostic support (imaging, tabular clinical data)
  • Low-resource and dialectal NLP / speech translation for telemedicine

Recent research
