Bangla Dialect Speech to Standard English Speech Translation System for Rural Telemedicine Application in Bangladesh

Khan, M. A. U., Gope, N., & Alam, G. R.

Manuscript submitted — IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems, IEEE

Research Focus

A Bangla dialect-to-English speech translation system for rural telemedicine, converting regional dialects to Standard Bangla before real-time English translation using LLMs (mBERT, T5), NMT (NMT5), and recurrent architectures (GRU, Bi-Directional LSTM, BARK), trained on a self-collected, multi-district dialect speech dataset.

My Contribution

Led the project as group lead from problem formulation to final validation, collecting fresh audio samples from native speakers across four districts (Chandpur, Brahmanbaria, Sylhet, Rangamati) after finding existing datasets insufficient for capturing dialectal nuance. Structured the dataset, created detailed CSV annotations, and performed preprocessing including noise filtering, speaker normalization, and dialect labeling. Implemented and fine-tuned the LLM (mBERT, T5), NMT (NMT5), and recurrent (GRU, Bi-Directional LSTM, BARK) pipelines, introducing targeted improvements to the translation architecture as the project progressed.
