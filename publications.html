Explainable churn prediction in telecom with tabular ML: Five model benchmark and SHAP analysis

Zerine, I., Islam, M. M., Khan, M. A. U., Chy, M. A. R., Saimon, A. S. M., Manik, M. M. T. G., & Wata, C.

Discover Artificial Intelligence · Vol. 6 · 263 · 2026

Research Focus

Explainable churn prediction benchmarking five tabular ML models on the Kaggle Telco Customer Churn dataset, with a multilayer perceptron reaching 92.28% accuracy and SHAP-based interpretability.

My Contribution
Modeling & Evaluation FrameworkCross-Validation & Statistical Testing

Led the modeling and evaluation framework in scikit-learn, wrapping all preprocessing (imputation, encoding, scaling) inside a single pipeline, implementing the multilayer perceptron and nested cross-validation with Wilcoxon signed-rank testing, compiled the primary figures and tables, and wrote the manuscript.
