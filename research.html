Research Interests

01

Trustworthy & Efficient AI for High-Impact Domains

Building efficient, scalable, and trustworthy AI systems for healthcare and other high-impact domains where data is noisy, imbalanced, and consequential. My work focuses on robust adaptation to real-world conditions, explainability that clinicians and stakeholders can trust, and secure model design so AI is not just accurate, but dependable where it matters most.

02

Real-Time Multilingual Multimodal Interaction

Designing AI systems that perceive, reason, and respond in real time across speech, text, and multimodal signals, including full-duplex voice agents and interaction models that don't just predict, but adapt and learn continuously from interaction rather than relying solely on static, pre-trained knowledge.

03

Generative AI & Responsible Foundation Models

Advancing generative AI and foundation models that reason responsibly. I'm particularly interested in trustworthy NLP: models that are transparent, robust to bias, and safe to deploy as a foundation for ethical, real-world AI systems.

Ongoing Research

DINOv3 ConvNeXt-B with lesion-adaptive multiscale feature fusion for dermoscopic skin lesion classification

Combining Lesion Aware Dual Attention, Adaptive Dilated Scale Fusion, and Cross Scale Gated Aggregation on a DINOv3 ConvNeXt-B backbone, evaluated on HAM10000 with external validation on ISIC 2017, PAD-UFES-20, and BCN20000.

Ongoing

Bangla dialect speech to standard English speech translation for rural telemedicine

A speech translation system converting regional Bangla dialects to Standard Bangla before real-time English translation, built on a self-collected, multi-district dialect speech dataset.

Ongoing · IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems

Privacy-preserving federated brain tumor segmentation

Extending nnU-Net and Swin-UNETR to multi-institution settings without centralizing patient data, evaluated on the BraTS 2020 dataset.

Ongoing · MICAD 2026, Edinburgh, UK
